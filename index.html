What is Vapigo?

Vapigo is a smart bike-taxi platform that connects riders with nearby captains for quick, affordable, and safe rides within the city.

How It Works

For Riders

Book a bike, get picked up fast, and reach your destination safely.

For Captains

Accept rides, earn daily, and work on your own schedule.

Why Vapigo?

  • Low fares
  • Quick pickups
  • Verified captains
  • Easy payments
